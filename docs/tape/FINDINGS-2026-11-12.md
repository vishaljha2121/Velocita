## The rebuilt books

All 12 books stayed valid on every voter, and no book went unknown. Integrity checks flagged 10 trades printed
outside the reconstructed best bid and offer (possibly timing between the TRADE and DEPTH channels, worth a
closer look) and 18 snapshots older than the book's position, which are ignored by design.

## The tape itself

One leader and term all hour, about 25 log entries committed per second, 56 signed tree heads. The tape sent
22 to 50 KB/s per host, 90 to 100% of each host's Tailscale traffic.

## The 19:14 incident

All three watchdogs stopped the tape at 19:14 UTC (1.8 to 2.9 MB/s for a minute). The burst began on NUC1, and
at first the tape's Aeron traffic was a small part of it; Aeron retransmissions (about 600 KB/s on NUC1) and
loss reports followed once packets were being dropped. About a minute earlier, at 19:12:36, NUC1's Grafana
logged a burst of failed session-token checks, the pattern of a browser opening a Grafana page there with a
stale login cookie from the other Grafana on the same host. Inference: a burst of non-tape traffic on the
shared Wi-Fi can still push the tape into amplifying loss, and the MTU fix (D-56) did not prevent that; the
watchdog contained it. Opening the dashboard as the trigger is a lead, not proven. Test: open the dashboard
once the tape is back and watch for a trip. If it trips again, cap Aeron's retransmission settings.
