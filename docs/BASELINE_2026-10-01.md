## What the baseline contains

The decisions and their reasoning are in ADRs 0001–0019. The incidents and fixes are in
M3_DEPLOYMENT_LOG findings 1–51.

- **Kernel**
  - Raft consensus over a pinned willemt fork with durable-quorum commits.
  - Pre-vote (CP-076), early durable acks, and compaction behind a replicated watermark.
  - Operator-only, position-bounded risk limits: long-only by default (ADR 0015).
  - The mass-cancel result is encodable (ADR 0007 amendment).
- **Strategy edge**
  - Strategy caller 21 is separate from operator caller 9 (ADR 0016). Paper mode re-syncs
    from refusals.
  - Volume strategies: churn, trend_churn, rotate, dip.
  - A weekly loss stop.
  - Market-data resilience, plus a source-generation re-sync (CP-077).
  - The candle price source for SOL.
- **Gateway** (ADR 0017): a separate process with authenticated, single-use authority
  proofs.
  - Persisted paper venue, zero-lot reconciliation, fence-only automatic re-arm (CP-078).
  - Paper venue: FOK at the measured costs (ADR 0018).
- **Live path:** plan and mock only (ADR 0019, `docs/LIVE_TRADING_JOURNEY.md`). No live
  transport is compiled into any build; the arming refusal was verified on a built image.
- **Observability:** five dashboards and 31 promtool-tested alert rules.
- **Operations**
  - Nightly market-data archive, with Mac backup and ack-gated pruning
    (`docs/MD_ARCHIVE_RUNBOOK.md`).
  - Clean-reset runbook (`docs/CLEAN_RESET_RUNBOOK.md`).
  - Paper trading runbook.
- **Research:** the volume battery (`docs/evidence/volume_battery_2026-09-30`). Every
  strategy buys about $86 of volume per $1 lost on cbBTC, and about $101 on SOL; none shows
  an edge.

## Known open items at the baseline

- **Alerts are not delivered anywhere** outside Grafana. Owner to choose; recommended:
  Telegram plus Healthchecks.io.
- **Pi:** a watchdog and a persistent journal are planned after this phase.
- **Mac:** the md-backup launchd job is not yet installed.
- **Kernel before live:** check each fill's fee and price, add a loss breaker, and add a
  flatten verb. True Markets venue contract questions (M5) are still open.
- **md-stream** hung silently for about 43 h (finding 50). It is now alerted on; the root
  cause is not investigated.
- **One-way link failures** are not covered: willemt has no CheckQuorum.
