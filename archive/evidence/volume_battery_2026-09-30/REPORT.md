## Caveats

- BTCUSDT on Binance is a proxy for cbBTC's True Markets reference price; the reference is a 30 s price whose construction is not documented, so short-horizon features (DEVS) may behave differently live.
- Costs are the mean of three quotes per side on one afternoon; the x0.8 / x1.25 columns bound them.
- The kernel re-sync is modelled as losing exactly one tick after a fill; killed or refused orders are assumed not to cost a tick (unverified).
- The weekly loss cap and weekly top-up are modelled operator rules; the kernel has no weekly loss limit.
- Ticks are exactly 30 s apart; live ticks wait for the next ~12 s True Markets refresh after the 30 s throttle, so live cadence is slower (about 36 s) and churn volume per hour lower; the weekly volume at the cap is unchanged, only the time to reach it.
- Runtime 961 s, 15 CPUs.
